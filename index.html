Aggregator / Partner � EIP-712 routed trades

Two-party flow: the user connects their wallet, approves the adapter for the trade amount plus adapter fee, and signs the typed trade authorization. The aggregator/partner then connects its own wallet and submits the signed package. The connected submitter is recorded in partner analytics. The signature is bound to this adapter, BNB Smart Chain (56), trade direction, amount, minimum output, fee cap, nonce and expiry.

1. User authorization

Connect the user's wallet, load the adapter and enter a trade amount to quote.

Safe EIP-1271 authorization

Safe owner signatures are collected for the SafeMessage that wraps the adapter digest. Each EOA owner can connect with WalletConnect and sign; contract owners can provide their EIP-1271 signature bytes. The assembled Safe signature is tested against the Safe�s isValidSignature(bytes32,bytes) before a package is created.
Prepare a trade to load Safe owners and threshold.

2. Partner submission

Connect the aggregator/partner wallet before submitting. The submitter address is the partner attribution key.
Important: user approval must be granted by the user's wallet before the partner submits. The partner wallet pays BNB gas. A partner must submit from its own wallet to be attributed separately. EOA authorizations use EIP-712 recovery. Safe authorizations use owner signatures packed in Safe format and verified by the Safe EIP-1271 interface before packaging. Partner fees are analytics only; the contract does not pay partner commissions.
